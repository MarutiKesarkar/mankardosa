OUR STORY

It began with
a cart. And a dream.

In 1986, Shrikant Mankar began serving dosas and uttapas from a hand cart on Satara Road. People came for the food. They came back for the taste.

That small beginning grew into one of Pune’s familiar South Indian names. Today, Santosh More and Dhiraj Mankar carry the Mankar tradition forward.
